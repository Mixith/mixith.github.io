About Me

Technical Artist Environment Artist Cat haver Frog enjoyer Silly goober

I'm a Technical Artist currently studying at The Game Assembly, with some previous experience in Environment art. I enjoy making shaders and creating tools to decrease repetitive tasks. In my free time I mostly find myself playing games, hanging out with friends, reading, and learning new things!

One of my favourite things in games is exploration. I love seeing all the game has to offer but I especially enjoy playing in unintended ways to see if I can somehow break levels by either skipping sections or jailbreaking my way out of the map.
