Ecopoint

This was my final game project at PlaygroundSquad before going out to internship. In Ecopoint you play as a researcher who is trying to uncover why the nature near them has grown corrupt and put an end to it once and for all.

My Contributions

Other Contributions

During this project I mostly focused on foliage, the majority of greenery you can see in the screenshots are stuff that I've made. I also made the water material, waterfall and modelled the bridges.
