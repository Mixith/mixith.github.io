Finders Keepers

Finders Keepers is my second group project at The Game Assembly. We worked in our own custom engine "Bean" to create a third person game where you play as the master thief Penny and are on a mission to steal back a precious item from the kind for a Baron.

My Contributions

Dash Bubble Shader

The dash bubbles we can see in game use a shader that I first prototyped in Unreal and then created in HLSL so we could use it in our own engine "Bean".

Bubble Popping

When the bubble pops there is a particle burts of smaller bubbles that I created and we can also see the dissolve part of the shader in action! This is controlled by a dissolve parameter that is changed by the programmers when the player jumps out of the bubble to make it disappaear and then reapper after a short while.

Other Contributions

During this project I also updated my Maya export plugin I made during the first game project so now the animators can choose their own export paths instead of it being hard coded to the project. I also made it give a warning at export in case the animators had removed namespaces in the workfile.

Besides that I also did a lot of tweaking and polishing on other VFX that I originally wasn't the creator of and did a bunch of playtesting to help our level designers find bugs and issues with their maps.
